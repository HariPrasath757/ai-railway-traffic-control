📖 Metric Definitions ▾
• Fleet Progress: % of total planned nominal route work completed.
• Remaining Work: Unfinished nominal travel time.
• Effective Completion: Sum of all train progress fractions.
• Throughput: Completed trains per sim hr.
• Delay: actualArrival - scheduledArrival (completed).
• Waiting Time: Real queued stoppage time.
• On-Time: Delay ≤ 5 min among completed.
• Utilization: Capacity-time integral ratio.
💡 Research Evaluation Methodology: For fixed-horizon railway traffic control, completed-train throughput alone is an incomplete measure. The framework evaluates the entire fleet state at the simulation horizon using Fleet Progress (%) and Remaining Journey Work (min) to accurately reflect unfinished train progression.
